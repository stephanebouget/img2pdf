A simple and efficient tool to convert your images into A4 portrait PDF documents, directly in your browser without uploading to any external servers.

## 🌟 Features

- ✅ **Local processing**: No images sent to the internet
- ✅ **A4 portrait format**: Optimized for standard printing
- ✅ **Multi-format support**: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP compatible
- ✅ **Drag & drop interface**: Intuitive user experience
- ✅ **Real-time preview**: See before you download
- ✅ **Free & open source**: No usage limitations

## 🚀 Usage

**Access the application directly:** [https://stephanebouget.github.io/img2pdf/](https://stephanebouget.github.io/img2pdf/)

1. Drag & drop your images or click to select them
2. Organize page order if needed
3. Click "Generate PDF" to download your document

## 🔒 Privacy

Your images remain **100% private**:
- Entirely client-side processing (in your browser)
- No uploads to servers
- No data collected or stored
